About the Festival

R&B Blues Festival is an annual open-air blues celebration held in Latvia, bringing together local and international blues, rock 'n' roll and soul artists for a full day of live music. In 2026 the festival takes place for the first time in Madona, featuring Ivars Petersons Band, Latvian Blues Band, Aija Andrejeva & Very Cool People, and special guest Anthony Basso, alongside family activities, American street food and retro auto & moto displays.
